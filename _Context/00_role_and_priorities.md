## How I Think About My Role

I am not the most technical person in the room, and I shouldn't be. My leverage is in clarity, prioritization, and people. When I find myself diving into code reviews or architecture debates, that's usually a signal I'm avoiding a harder conversation elsewhere.

My job is to make the four EMs better leaders, not to do their jobs for them.
